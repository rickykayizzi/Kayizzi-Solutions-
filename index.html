About KayizziSolutions

KayizziSolutions is an independent technology-help project focused on practical digital problems in Uganda. Our goal is to publish useful, understandable guides, distinguish verified facts from common troubleshooting suggestions, and update information when it changes.

Always confirm current bundles, APN details and account-specific issues with your network provider.
