Wi-Fi Connected but No Internet on Android: How to Fix It

The Wi-Fi icon can show that your phone is connected to a router even when the router has no internet connection. These checks help identify whether the problem is the phone, the Wi-Fi network, or the internet provider.

For Uganda · Reviewed 23 October 2026 · Independent guide

Stay safe: Never share your mobile money PIN, password, or one-time verification code with anyone offering technical help.

1. Test another app or website

Open a browser and try more than one website. If only one app or site fails, the problem may be specific to that service.

2. Turn Wi-Fi off and on

Turn Wi-Fi off, wait a few seconds, and turn it back on. Reconnect to the intended network.

3. Toggle Airplane mode and restart

Turn Airplane mode on for about 10 seconds and off again. If the connection still fails, restart the phone.

4. Check another device

Connect another phone or laptop to the same Wi-Fi network. If no device can access the internet, the router or provider is a more likely cause. If other devices work, focus on the affected phone.

5. Restart the router safely

If it is your home router and you are authorized to manage it, unplug its power, wait around 30 seconds, then reconnect it. Allow it to finish starting up. Do not hold the factory-reset button unless you intend to erase its configuration.

6. Move closer to the router

Walls, distance, and interference can weaken Wi-Fi. Test near the router. If the connection works nearby but not in another room, coverage may be the issue.

7. Check for a sign-in page

Some public, hotel, school, or business networks require you to accept terms or sign in through a browser. Follow legitimate sign-in instructions. Never enter mobile money PINs or unrelated passwords into a suspicious Wi-Fi page.

8. Forget and reconnect

In Wi-Fi settings, select the network and choose Forget, then reconnect using the correct password. Only do this if you know the password; forgetting removes saved credentials.

9. Check date, VPN, and Private DNS

Set date and time automatically. If you use a VPN or custom Private DNS, temporarily turn it off to test whether it is causing the issue, then restore your preferred setting. Do not change advanced settings you do not understand.

10. Reset network settings as a last resort

If the phone still cannot connect to any Wi-Fi network, consider the network-reset option in Settings. Read the warning first because saved Wi-Fi passwords and Bluetooth pairings may be removed. This is not the same as a factory reset.

When to contact your provider

If every device on your home network is offline after restarting the router, check its status lights and contact your internet provider. If the problem happens only on one phone across several networks, consult the phone maker or a qualified technician.
