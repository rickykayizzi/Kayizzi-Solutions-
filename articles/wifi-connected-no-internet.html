Wi-Fi Connected but No Internet? How to Troubleshoot

For Uganda · Independent guide · Last checked 24 October 2026

A Wi-Fi icon means your phone is connected to a router or hotspot; it does not guarantee that the router has working internet access.

Try these checks in order

  1. Open another website or app to rule out a single-service outage.
  2. Check whether other devices on the same Wi-Fi can access the internet.
  3. Turn Wi-Fi off and on, then reconnect.
  4. Restart your phone.
  5. Check the router's power and internet indicators. Avoid pressing reset unless you know the configuration details.
  6. If you control the router, unplug power for about 30 seconds, reconnect and allow it to restart.
  7. If a login portal is used, open a browser and complete sign-in.
  8. If only one phone is affected, forget the network and reconnect using the correct password.
  9. If every device is offline, check provider status or contact support.

When to contact your provider

Contact your ISP if the router indicates no service, all devices are offline or the issue repeatedly returns. Do not post your Wi-Fi password publicly.
