TakeNow Uganda: Forgot Your Phone Loan Reference Number?

For Uganda · Independent guide · Last checked 24 October 2026

If you bought a phone through TakeNow and forgot your payment reference or account details, do not guess a reference number or send money to an unverified person.

Protect your account: Never share your mobile-money PIN, phone unlock password, OTP or full identity documents with an unofficial helper.

What to check first

  1. Search SMS messages for the original loan approval, payment instructions or payment confirmation.
  2. Check the purchase paperwork, receipt or agreement from the authorised seller.
  3. Review mobile-money transaction messages for previous repayments. Keep transaction IDs private when posting publicly.
  4. Take a screenshot of any lock-screen message, but cover sensitive details before sharing it.

If you still cannot find the reference

Contact TakeNow using the customer-care details in your agreement, official app or verified official channel. Explain that you forgot the account/payment reference and ask them to confirm it securely. We could not verify a current official public self-service recovery page, so this guide deliberately does not invent a USSD code or phone number.

If your TakeNow phone is locked

  1. Read the lock-screen message carefully.
  2. Confirm recent repayments from mobile-money transaction history.
  3. Connect to trusted internet if the device-management system needs to refresh.
  4. Contact TakeNow through the verified channel and ask them to check account status.
  5. Do not factory-reset the phone or install bypass tools; these may not remove the finance lock and could risk your data.

Can KayizziSolutions help?

We can explain the general process and help you prepare questions. We cannot retrieve private account records, issue references or unlock a financed phone for the provider.
