Mobile Data Not Working on Android? Try These 10 Fixes

When mobile data stops working, the cause may be a setting, SIM selection, exhausted bundle, weak coverage, or a carrier issue. Work through these checks in order before resetting anything.

For Uganda · Reviewed 23 October 2026 · Independent guide

Stay safe: Never share your mobile money PIN, password, or one-time verification code with anyone offering technical help.

1. Confirm mobile data is on

Open Settings and look for Mobile data or Cellular data. Turn it on. If already on, turn it off, wait a few seconds, and turn it on again.

2. Select the correct SIM

On a dual-SIM phone, open SIM or Mobile Network settings and check which SIM is selected for mobile data. Calls can use one SIM while data uses the other.

3. Check your bundle

Confirm that your data bundle is active and has remaining data. The exact balance-check method depends on your provider and can change; use the provider’s official app, menu, or support page rather than relying on an old code from an unverified post.

4. Toggle Airplane mode

Turn Airplane mode on, wait about 10 seconds, then turn it off. Give the phone a moment to reconnect.

5. Restart your phone

Save your work, restart the phone, and test a website again. A restart can clear a temporary software or network-registration problem.

6. Check signal and coverage

Move outdoors or to another location and test again. Look for a data indicator such as 4G, LTE, 3G, or H. Signal bars alone do not guarantee that mobile data is available.

7. Test more than one app

Open a browser and try two different websites. If only one app fails, update it or check whether its service is having a problem.

8. Review APN settings

Open Settings and search for APN or Access Point Names. Select the correct SIM and use the profile supplied by your operator. If the APN was recently changed, restore the default profile if available. Do not copy advanced settings from another country or carrier.

9. Check data restrictions

Data Saver, per-app data restrictions, or a usage limit can prevent some apps from using mobile data. Review Settings → Network/Connections → Data usage. Menu names vary by device.

10. Reset network settings only if needed

Look for Reset network settings or Reset Wi-Fi, mobile & Bluetooth. Read the confirmation screen: saved Wi-Fi networks, Bluetooth pairings, and other preferences may be removed. Avoid a full factory reset as an early troubleshooting step.

When to contact your provider

Contact your operator if mobile data fails in multiple locations, your SIM cannot register for data, or others nearby have the same issue. Ask them to check your line, bundle, local coverage, and known outages.

Reviewed 23 October 2026. Steps are based on Google’s Android connection guidance; menu names differ by phone.
